# University Hostel Management System Design Rule

Always adhere to the **Prestige Academic** design system specified in `docs/design_system.md`:

1. **Color Palette**:
   - Institutional Brand: Oxford Navy `#0B192C` and `#1E3E62` with Academic Gold `#D97706`.
   - Work Canvas: Ceramic Slate `#F8FAFC`, Pristine White Cards `#FFFFFF`, and Subtle Borders `#E2E8F0`.
   - High Legibility Text: Slate Charcoal `#0F172A` (primary), Slate `#475569` (secondary), `#94A3B8` (muted).
   - Semantic Badges: Success `#16A34A`, Warning `#D97706`, Danger `#DC2626`, Info `#2563EB`.

2. **UI Guidelines**:
   - Do not default to generic pitch-black gamer/hacker neon themes. Use authoritative, clean institutional styling.
   - Use Title Case for headings; do not shout with all-caps labels or arbitrary acronyms.
   - Ground layouts with double-buffering, rounded corners (10–16px), and clean padding.
   - Enforce genuine database integration: all diagnostic steppers, KPI numbers, and dropdowns calculate from real database records.
